📦 INSTALLATION

1. Extract the ZIP file

2. Install dependencies:
   pip install -r requirements.txt

3. Run the app:
   python app.py

4. Open browser:
   http://localhost:5000

🎯 COMPLETE USER FLOW

1. CREATE ACCOUNT
   ─────────────
   • Go to http://localhost:5000 (redirects to login)
   • Click "Sign Up" tab
   • Enter your details:
     - Full Name
     - Username (unique, min 3 chars)
     - Password (min 6 chars)
   • Click "Create Account"
   • You're automatically logged in!

2. TAKE ASSESSMENT
   ────────────────
   • Select your profile type (undergraduate, graduate, etc.)
   • Complete 5-step survey about your background
   • Answer adaptive quiz questions (8-10 questions)
   • View your personalized career results
   • Explore learning roadmaps with free resources

3. VIEW YOUR DASHBOARD
   ───────────────────
   • Click "📊 My Dashboard" in navigation (top right)
   • See your profile card with:
     - Avatar (your initials)
     - Full name and username
     - Join date
   
   • Check your statistics:
     - Total assessments taken
     - Resources completed
     - Latest career path recommended
   
   • Review your assessment history:
     - All past assessments
     - Career recommended each time
     - Dates and scores

4. RETAKE ASSESSMENT
   ─────────────────
   • From dashboard, click "← Take Assessment"
   • Your new results are added to your history
   • Previous results are preserved
   • Compare your journey over time

5. TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
   ───────────────────
   • Database tracks your resource completion
   • As you complete courses/resources, they're saved
   • View your progress in the dashboard
